# Feature Catalog

[Documentation index](../README.md#documentation) · [Feature index](README.md) · [Architecture](../architecture/system-overview.md) · [Operations](../guides/operations.md)

HDGUI is a local, single-user web interface for exploring geocentric planetary positions alongside the Human Design gate system. A separate `humandesign_api` service supplies calculations; HDGUI presents results and manages local profiles, settings and cached data.

## Transit exploration [transit-exploration]

- **Current transit BodyGraph (`/`)** shows gates, channels and centers derived from the current transit only, with a timestamp and position table.
- **Transit wheel (`/transit`)** presents planetary positions in the zodiac and the 64-gate sequence.
- **Transit refresh controls (`/settings`)** configure the snapshot cache lifetime and optional page autoreload. Refreshes produce timestamped snapshots, not continuous planetary motion.
- **Gate-change estimates** search future calculation samples and account for retrograde motion. They are estimates with finite sampling resolution and search limits, not guaranteed exact transition times or personal predictions.

## Birth profiles and saved charts [birth-profiles]

- **Profile management (`/profiles`)** stores birth time, timezone and place label locally, and lets the user select one current profile.
- **Saved charts** retain profile BodyGraph SVGs. Natal activations are calculated and cached separately when needed.
- **Complete natal snapshots (`/profiles/{profile_id}/analysis`)** retain validated API details, activation substructure and catalog links separately from SVGs and editable catalog descriptions. The profile chart page displays the BodyGraph beside the principal catalog-linked properties, variables and center status; missing or stale details can be recalculated there.
- **Profile overlays** can highlight profile gates in the gate catalog and show fixed personality/design reference bands on a sampled timeline.
- **Personal and transit data remain distinct** in the home BodyGraph: selecting a profile does not merge its activations into the current transit chart.

## Timeline analysis [timeline-analysis]

- **Gate timeline (`/experiments/gate-plot`)** provides a zoomable, sampled view of gate and line changes, with optional fixed natal reference bands.
- **Timeline sampling (`/experiments/timeline`, `/experiments/frame`)** validates a time window and loads calculations for requested UTC timestamps. Loaded samples can be reused by timeline, ephemeris, series and gate-change searches.
- **Ephemeris and transit series (`/experiments/ephemeris`, `/experiments/series`)** show explicitly requested position samples and series.
- **Sampling is discrete.** Lines connect neighboring successful samples; they do not prove every intermediate state, and short transitions can be missed.

## Personal center timeline [center-timeline]

The **center timeline (`/centers`)** combines the selected profile's natal activations with transit activations at a selectable UTC timestamp. It displays the resulting BodyGraph beside nine independently selectable sampled center-definition tracks. Selecting a time requests a calculation for that time rather than snapping the graph to the nearest sample.

The tracks reuse bounded samples. Unloaded gaps remain visible, short transitions may be missed, and a hanging gate alone does not define a center. This combined view is separate from the transit-only home BodyGraph.

## Catalogs and learning [catalogs-learning]

- **Gate and channel catalogs (`/gates`, `/channels`)** connect the 64 gates, 36 channels and seven circuits, with optional highlighting from the selected profile.
- **HD entity catalog (`/knowledge/entities`)** lists the structured Human Design vocabulary and 384 gate-line text slots. Descriptions are separately imported and retain per-entry authorship and review provenance.
- **Gate walk (`/experiments/gate-walk`)** is a geometric learning animation that follows the zodiac order and gate-center relationships. It is not a planetary simulation or chronological playback.
- Catalog descriptions are local text data and may be explicitly imported; the catalog's structural relationships are application data.

## Settings and local data [settings-data]

HDGUI keeps profiles, saved charts, settings and persistent calculation samples in its local database. In-memory caches are process-local. There are no accounts or profile ownership controls, so the application is intended to remain on localhost. See [Operations](../guides/operations.md) for configuration, migrations, backup and data-handling details.

## Not delivered [not-delivered]

The application does not provide continuous real-time or accelerated planetary playback, automatic natal–transit synthesis in the home chart, simultaneous multi-profile comparison, public multi-user access, or a validated public-hosting security boundary. These should not be inferred from the gate-walk animation, profile overlays or center timeline.

Astrology and Human Design are interpretive frameworks, not scientifically established explanations of personality or life events. Computed positions do not validate personal predictions, and the application is not a diagnostic or treatment tool.
